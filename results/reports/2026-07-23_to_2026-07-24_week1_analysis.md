## 1. Cited domains, ranked and categorized

210 responses cited **332 distinct domains**. The vast majority (~280) are
one-off citations of individual competitor swim-school websites — not
realistic listing or mention targets on their own. Below are the domains that
repeat often enough, or are structurally the right *type* of site, to be
worth Swimingo's effort. Verified against the actual URLs cited (not just
the bare domain name) before categorizing.

## 2. Worst-answered questions (Week 6 answer-bank seed)

Method: started from responses that named neither Swimingo nor a tracked
competitor, then read each one in full to separate genuine failures from
answers that were simply fine-but-unbranded (e.g. several ChatGPT answers
named 6-8 real local businesses by name that just aren't in our competitor
list — those are citation *gaps*, not bad answers, and are already captured
in the summary report's Gaps section). These five survived that filter:

1. **Q18 — "how much does an adult swim instructor cost in Aurora" (cost) — WRONG, corroborated on 2 platforms.**
   Gemini answered about **Aurora, Colorado** (cites "City of Aurora's... Moorhead Recreation Center"). Perplexity answered about **Aurora, Illinois** (cites a DuPage-area swim center). Neither is Aurora, Ontario — the two platforms even disagree with each other on which wrong city. This is the most damaging failure type: a user could act on real-looking pricing for the wrong market entirely.

2. **Q42 — "can I watch my child's swim lesson" (trust) — VAGUE, ChatGPT only.**
   At 554 characters, roughly a fifth the length of its own answers to similar questions, it ends by asking the user to supply a specific swim school before it can help — a non-answer. (Gemini and Perplexity both gave substantive, useful answers to the same question, so this is ChatGPT-specific, not a hole in the question itself.)

3. **Q30 — "at-home swim lessons for kids in Burnaby" (discovery) — VAGUE, Gemini.**
   Names zero specific companies; tells the user to search Facebook groups ("Burnaby Moms," "Burnaby Parents") and Nextdoor instead of recommending anyone.

4. **Q34 — "private swim instructors that come to your home in Winnipeg" (discovery) — VAGUE, Gemini.**
   Same pattern: recommends searching Kijiji and Facebook Marketplace rather than naming a provider.

5. **Q36 — "at-home swim lessons for kids in Windsor Ontario" (discovery) — VAGUE, Gemini.**
   Same pattern again: points to Kijiji/Facebook search terms instead of a named business.

Note the pattern: 3 of 5 are Gemini defaulting to "go search yourself" for
smaller markets (Burnaby, Winnipeg, Windsor) rather than naming any provider
— a distinguishable, structural weak spot in Gemini's grounding for
lower-population cities, not a one-off. These 5 (discovery/cost questions
where a confident, correct, named-provider answer is the reasonable
expectation) are the actionable set. A separate observation: the fully
generic questions (Q38, Q40, Q41 — "are private lessons safer," "what should
I have ready," "will my child have the same instructor") were answered well
by every platform but never mention any brand by nature of the question —
that's not a quality failure, but it is still real answer-bank
white space worth a Week 6 FAQ page, just a different opportunity than the 5
above.
